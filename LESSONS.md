# Synthesis Tutor fractions lessons

1. introduces cookie game. split 4 cookies between 2 people (teaches basic division)
2. split 3 cookies between 4 people (introduces cutting for the first time, the concept of a fraction)
3. splitting 4 cookies between 3 people (introduces splitting into thirds)
4. 7 cookies between 2 people (introduces mixed numbers)
5. split 4 halves between 2 people (teaches the equivalence of 1 and 2/2)
6. 7 cookies among 4 people (harder problem forcing you to split so its 1 + 1/2 + 1/4)
7. introduces new game of coloring squares. 5 out of 8 squares are red, write this as a fraction
